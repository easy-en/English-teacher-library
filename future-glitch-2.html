THE FUTURE GLITCH 2.0

Every choice changes your world. Every sentence changes your future.

The timeline has collapsed. Two young time travelers must build a new universe, make hard decisions with First and Second Conditionals, and unlock the secret word.

Mission briefing

  • 🌍Create your universe. Choose an environment, a companion and the personality of your world.
  • 🧩Solve 16 challenges in 4 chapters. Some have one correct answer. In decision challenges, you choose your plan, but one sentence has a grammar mistake. Find a correct one!
  • 🌆Watch your world change. Every correct answer changes the scene on the right and the final ending.
  • 🔤Collect 6 secret letters. Put them in the right order after challenge 16.
  • 🤝Play together. Take turns. Nobody is out of the game! Wrong answers only mean “try again”.

FIRST CONDITIONAL

If + Present Simple, will + verb
If we repair the generator, our base will have power again.

SECOND CONDITIONAL

If + Past Simple, would + verb
If I could control the weather, I would protect the planet.
